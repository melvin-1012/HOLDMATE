Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first pickup.

How does luggage pickup work?

Book a pickup window through our app or site, and a Holdmate courier arrives at your chosen location — home, hotel, or office — to collect your bags in person.

Is my luggage insured while in storage?

Yes. Every bag is covered under our standard insurance policy for the full duration of storage and transit, with optional add-on coverage for high-value items.

How accurate is the live tracking?

Each bag is fitted with a GPS-enabled tag, giving you location updates accurate to a few meters, visible on the map in real time.

Can I change my delivery address after booking?

Absolutely — you can update your delivery location any time before the courier is dispatched, directly from your dashboard or by calling support.

Which cities does Holdmate operate in?

We currently operate in over 120 cities worldwide, with new locations added every month. Check the app for coverage in your destination.
